MaintChain

Every repair,
provably tamper-proof.

A multi-party compliance platform for industrial maintenance records, powered by Stellar Soroban smart contracts.

The Problem

Industrial maintenance records cost industries $10B+/year in fraud

Mutability

Paper logs and spreadsheets can be altered after the fact. No trusted historical record.

Single-Party Trust

One person's approval is rarely audited. Single point of failure in compliance chains.

Siloed Reputation

A technician's work history doesn't travel. Every employer starts from zero trust.

Expensive Audits

Verifying repair history requires chasing siloed records across organizations.

The Solution

Multi-party cryptographic approval on Stellar

To fake a single record, an attacker would need to compromise 3 independent keys, fabricate evidence hashes, and create false equipment lineage. The cost of collusion exceeds any plausible benefit.

6-Stage Compliance Flow

Detection → Assignment → Evidence → Verification → Approval → Certificate

Every step is permanently recorded on the Stellar blockchain.
